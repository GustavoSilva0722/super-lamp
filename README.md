# Temple of the Lost Wilds

A first-person, solo, arcade-style endless runner in a low-poly fantasy wilderness with a pulp-adventure
(Indiana Jones-style) theme. Run through ancient ruins, leap logs and pits, slide under stone beams,
swing across chasms on ropes, grab golden idols, and outrun the boulder.

- **Visible boulder**: watch it in the "Behind You!" rear-view mirror; every mistake lets it gain on you.
- **Rope swings**: when a chasm appears, press jump near the hanging rope to swing across.
- **High score** saved in your browser, intense procedural adventure music (press M to mute).

Open `index.html` in a browser (needs internet for the Three.js CDN), or serve it: `python3 -m http.server`.

Controls: A/D or ←/→ change lane · W/↑/Space jump (and grab the rope) · S/↓ slide · M music · touch: swipe.
