Food waste hackathon · Challenge 3

Tinder for surplus food

Problem

Shops, restaurants, hotels and caterers throw away good food every day, often whole buffets, while small charities nearby need it.

Root cause

Good food goes to waste because businesses can't find someone to take it in time. There is no quick way to see which charity nearby has space, a fridge and a volunteer free right now, so the food is binned.

Solution

Givers post food in seconds. Rescuers only see food they can really use, and one swipe makes a match. Subscribe to a favourite giver and their food comes to you automatically.

Presenter controls

Open the charity first, then press "Bakery posts food now" to see the notification arrive. "Show a live delivery" starts a courier on the map. On the swipe screen you can use the ← → ↑ keys.
